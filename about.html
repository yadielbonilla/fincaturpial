About

Finca Turpial brings together quality produce, tropical plants, and thoughtfully sourced consumables for a healthier lifestyle.

Our Story

At Finca Turpial, we work with trusted growers and carefully selected products that reflect freshness, sustainability, and the rich flavor of locally inspired agriculture. We focus on produce and plant varieties that are both useful and beautiful for every home and garden.

From sweet fruits and vibrant vegetables to specialty dragonfruit plants and pantry essentials, our goal is to make healthy, high-quality living more accessible to families and food lovers alike.

What We Offer

  • Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Dragonfruit plants for tropical gardens
  • Household and consumable pantry essentials
  • Seasonal selections and reliable quality
